= ESS Limit Total Charge

Limits the charge power of an energy storage system according to its State of Charge (SoC).

* Below `maxSoc` (default 85%), no additional power constraint is applied.
* At or above `maxSoc`, further charging is blocked.
* At or above `forceDischargeSoc` (default 90%), discharging is forced. A positive `forceDischargePower` is used as configured; otherwise one fifth of the available discharge power is used.
* State changes have a five-minute hysteresis.
* The energy scheduler limits charging to the energy corresponding to `maxSoc`.

`TARGET_AC` (default) constrains inverter power. For a hybrid ESS, `TARGET_DC` constrains battery power by adding local PV production, calculated as `max(0, ActivePower - DcDischargePower)`. Each missing AC or DC measurement defaults to zero, matching the ESS Limit Total Discharge controller. For a non-hybrid ESS both modes have the same effect.

Configure the component as `Controller.Ess.LimitTotalCharge` and place it before other power controllers in the scheduler so that its lower power bound is respected.
