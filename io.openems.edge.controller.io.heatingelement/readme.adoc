= IO Heating Element

Controls one or two three-phase heating elements via relay outputs according to grid active power.

== Configuration and Step Model

The controller supports the following configurations:

* `ONE`: one heating element, 3 relay outputs and Levels 0 to 3
* `TWO`: two heating elements, 6 relay outputs and Levels 0 to 6

Each heating element has its own configured power per phase.
Therefore, Heating Element A and Heating Element B may have different power values.

The configuration is converted once into an ordered list of relay steps.
The remaining controller logic works with this list, independently of the number of heating elements.

[source,text]
----
                                  Config
                                     |
                 +-------------------+-------------------+
                 |                                       |
                ONE                                     TWO
                 |                                       |
       3 output channels                       6 output channels
       power per phase A                 power per phase A and B
                                                distribution
                 |                                       |
                 +-------------------+-------------------+
                                     |
                                     v
                           buildHeatingSteps()
                                     |
                                     v
                       List<HeatingElementStep>
                                     |
                    +----------------+----------------+
                    |                |                |
                    v                v                v
               applyLevel()    power per Level   target Level
----

== Distribution, Levels and Power

A *Level* represents the number of active relay steps.
Level N means that the first N steps of the configured order are switched on.

For `ONE`, the order is always:

[source,text]
----
A-L1 -> A-L2 -> A-L3
----

For `TWO`, the order is selected by the configured distribution:

* `SEQUENTIAL`: `A-L1 -> A-L2 -> A-L3 -> B-L1 -> B-L2 -> B-L3`
* `PARALLEL`: `A-L1 -> B-L1 -> A-L2 -> B-L2 -> A-L3 -> B-L3`

The required power of a Level is the sum of the nominal powers of its active steps.
It is not calculated as `Level * powerPerPhase`.

Example with A = 2000 W per phase and B = 1000 W per phase:

[cols="1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Level |SEQUENTIAL |PARALLEL

|0 |0 W |0 W
|1 |2000 W |2000 W
|2 |4000 W |3000 W
|3 |6000 W |5000 W
|4 |7000 W |6000 W
|5 |8000 W |8000 W
|6 |9000 W |9000 W
|===

== Switching Behaviour

The same switching rules apply to both the 4-level and the 7-level configuration:

* `OFF`: switch immediately to Level 0.
* `FAST_HEAT`: switch immediately to the maximum available Level.
* `SURPLUS`:
** when reducing power, switch immediately to the required lower Level;
** when increasing power, activate at most one additional Level after the configured `minimumSwitchingTime`.

The switching delay therefore applies between two consecutive switch-on operations.
It does not delay switching off or switching to a lower Level.

== Meter and Calibration

A meter is optional.

With `ONE`, the existing behaviour remains unchanged:

* with a meter, the existing calibration and average-power calculation is used;
* without a meter, the configured power per phase is used.

With `TWO`, one common meter may be configured for both heating elements.
Automatic per-phase calibration is not used.
The nominal power of every step is always derived from the separately configured power-per-phase values of Heating Elements A and B.

== Legacy Modes and Schedule

The existing `Mode`, `WorkMode`, `defaultLevel` and `schedule` configuration remains supported for backward compatibility.
This includes the existing `NONE`, `TIME` and `ENERGY` work modes and the ability to select a specific `defaultLevel`.

For `TWO`, the same logic also accepts Levels 4 to 6. Scheduled Level values are limited to the maximum Level of the configured variant.
The schedule uses JSCalendar format and takes effect in automatic mode with the supported work modes.

== Water Temperature Control
Optionally, the controller can take the water temperature of a Thermometer into account. This is enabled via the parameter _Has Water Thermometer?_ and the _Water Thermometer-ID_. If it is disabled, the temperature is ignored and the controller works as described above.

In the Mode Automatic, the following rules are applied from top to bottom:

. *Thermometer not available:* the Level configured in _Sensor Error Level_ is applied immediately (_Level 0_ switches the heating element off).
. *Maximum Water Temperature* reached: the heating element is switched off immediately (safety limit).
. *Legionella Protection* (optional): if the water was not kept at or above the _Legionella Water Temperature_ for _Minimum Legionella Time_ within the last _Legionella Interval_ days, the heating element heats with the highest available Level (Level 3 with one, Level 6 with two heating elements), even without excess power, until the temperature was held for the minimum time. If the temperature drops below the _Legionella Water Temperature_ in between, the time starts again. To avoid heating up to the maximum, the heating element is switched off above the _Legionella Water Temperature_ + 2 K and heats again below. If the temperature is not held within _Maximum Legionella Heating Time_, the heating is aborted, the Channel _LegionellenProtectionFailed_ is set and the heating is retried after one day.
. *High Water Temperature* reached: the heating element is switched off immediately, even with excess power or while the minimum run time of the Work-Mode is not reached.
. Below *Low Water Temperature*: the heating element heats with the highest available Level, even without excess power.
. Between _Low_ and _High Water Temperature_: normal operation, i.e. excess power and Work-Mode as described above.

The limits _Maximum_, _High_ and _Sensor Error_ are applied immediately and do not wait for the _Minimum switching time_. Heating because of _Low Water Temperature_ or _Legionella Protection_ has priority over the Work-Mode and the schedule, but follows the switching behaviour described above, i.e. the Level is raised by one step per _Minimum switching time_. A _Sensor Error Level_ above the highest available Level is limited to the highest available Level.

In the Modes Manual On and Fast Heat the heating element heats with the _Default Level_ or all Levels respectively, unless the _High Water Temperature_ is reached (off) or the thermometer is not available (_Sensor Error Level_). In the Mode Manual Off the heating element is always off.

The configuration is validated on activation: _Low_ must be below _High_, _High_ must not be above _Maximum Water Temperature_, the _Legionella Water Temperature_ must not be above _Maximum Water Temperature_ and the _Maximum Legionella Heating Time_ must be greater than the _Minimum Legionella Time_.

Note: The time of the next Legionella heating is kept in memory only. After a restart of the Edge the interval starts again.

Channel _WaterActual_:: Current water temperature in [°C]

Channel _WaterTarget_:: Target water temperature in [°C]: _High Water Temperature_, or the _Legionella Water Temperature_ during Legionella heating

Channel _WaterThermometerNotAvailable_:: Warning if the configured Thermometer is not available

Channel _LegionellenProtectionFailed_:: Warning if the Legionella temperature was not held in time; it is reset when the Legionella protection was completed
